# Successful reel patterns (one-off reels)

Reference example: `reference_reels/tesla-roadster-2026-10-16/`. This is a pattern to test, not a formula.
Last updated: 2026-10-16 (1 example).

## Observed (facts from the logs; see performance_log.csv)
- Tesla Roadster reel (one-off, 59.7 s): Instagram, 7.5 h after posting: 1,767 views, 1,560 reach, 21 likes, 13 shares, 1 save, average watch 15.5 s.
- Our daily reels on Instagram at 35-180 h: 69-304 views, 41-261 reach, 0-6 likes, 0 shares, average watch 4.3-8.2 s.
- So on Instagram the Tesla reel had about 6-25x the views of a daily reel in a fraction of the time, about 2-3x the watch time, and the only meaningful share count.
- Pankaj reports strong results on TikTok and YouTube too: numbers NOT yet captured.
- Not observed/unknown: completion rate, profile visits, followers gained from this reel.

## What this example had (the characteristics to reuse and test)
1. Timely, widely discussed technology story (reveal the night before).
2. Cold open on real, relevant footage with its original sound (4.5 s), then a clear curiosity hook.
3. An engineering explanation that adds something beyond the news (how the thrusters and suction work).
4. A reality check separating what was shown from what is claimed or promised.
5. A natural comment question ("Would you trust a rocket-powered Tesla on the road?").
6. Fast pacing, readable captions, labelled overlays at the top, visible source credit.
7. Low-cost production with the existing voice, captions and FFmpeg tools; no paid generation.

## Hypotheses (not proven; confidence is low until we have more examples)
- H1: Real brand footage in the first seconds earns attention that stock footage does not. Test: same story style with stock only vs real footage.
- H2: The topic's pre-existing virality (Tesla, with 1M+ likes on the source clip) explains a large part of the reach. Test: a less famous brand with the same structure.
- H3: The reality check and the question drive shares/comments. Test: compare share counts when it is missing.
- H4: The longer 60 s length did not hurt because the footage kept changing. Test: a 45 s version of the same structure.
- H5: Soft 576 px phone-recorded footage did not stop it working, but higher-resolution sources may help.

## Risks to remember
- Reused brand footage can be taken down or limit monetization. Short clips, commentary, credit, press-page check. Pankaj decides.
- Do not assume followers gained are relevant to our AI-engineering niche: check follower conversion and who the audience is.
