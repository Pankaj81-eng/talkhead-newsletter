# Fact-check: Tesla Roadster reveal reel (checked 2026-10-16)

The reveal livestream was on 15 Oct 2026 (7:55 PM CT). Tesla posted the clips on Instagram (@teslamotors).

| Claim in the video | Status | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| The Roadster launches off a ramp and flips (Tesla calls it the "kickflip") | SHOWN in Tesla's own video; reported by several outlets. Not independently verified | Tesla Instagram clips; Electrek 15 Oct; autoevolution |
| Rocket version uses cold-gas thrusters (compressed gas), developed with SpaceX | REPORTED / Tesla listing. Details (e.g. 80 kN thrust, nitrogen tank) are unverified, so not stated in the video | Electrek; autoevolution; The Information (Aug 2026) |
| A suction system holds the car to a ramp upside down | CLAIMED by Tesla ("multi-mode suction"), shown in video, not independently verified | autoevolution; Autonocion |
| The flip was a prototype stunt with nobody inside | REPORTED (nobody inside the aerial prototype) | autoevolution (prototype with nobody inside) |
| Rocket version not approved for public roads | Tesla's own listing / website wording | Electrek; Autonocion; Tesla site |
| Zero to sixty in under one second (Tesla claim, 0.81 s live) | TESLA CLAIM, not independently verified; video says exactly that | Electrek; autoevolution |
| Production starts in 2028 | STATED by Musk/Tesla; the video calls it a target, not a guarantee | autoevolution; Electrek |

## Open uncertainties (not stated as fact in the video)
- Whether the flying/rolling footage was live or pre-produced: Autonocion argues it was a video, not live. Electrek treats it as real but unverified.
- Price, power figures, range, exact thrust and tank details: unconfirmed, left out.
- The video never says the car "can fly" as a fact. The on-screen "TESLA CAN FLY?" is a question; the script says it launches and flips.
