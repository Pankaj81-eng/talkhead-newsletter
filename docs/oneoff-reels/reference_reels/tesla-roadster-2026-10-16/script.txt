Tesla just showed a Roadster that launches off a ramp and flips through the air. But the engineering behind it is even more interesting than the video. This isn't a normal flying car. The Rocket version uses cold gas thrusters, powerful jets of compressed gas, developed with SpaceX. A separate suction system even holds the car to a ramp upside down. So is this a road car? Not yet. The flip was a prototype stunt with nobody inside, the Rocket version isn't approved for public roads, and Tesla's zero to sixty claim of under one second hasn't been independently verified. Musk says production starts in twenty twenty-eight, but that's a target, not a guarantee. Would you trust a rocket-powered Tesla on the road?
