## 2026-10-26 (later): blur check for stock clips
- Safe point before: tag `stable-2026-10-26-pre-blur-check`.
- Why: 25 Oct's delivery-robot video showed ~9 s of an out-of-focus crowd (stock clip `bf94e861...`); nothing measured sharpness.
- `factory/media/sharpness.py`: `clip_is_blurry` cuts 4 frames into a 4x3 grid and measures the Laplacian variance of the sharpest tiles (90th percentile), so a sharp subject on a soft background passes. Rule: blurry when p90 < 20 and mean brightness >= 60, or p90 < 6. Calibrated on all 144 cached clips: 5 flagged (blurry crowd, bokeh lights, blank sky, two nearly black frames), Sunday's blurry beat flagged, delivery-robot / white-cane / doctor clips pass. A first idea (blur-vs-sharp ratio) failed on real data and was dropped.
- `factory/media/assemble.py`: the check runs before the vision check in `_stock_fits` (multi-cut beats) and on the single-clip stock path. `factory/media/shotplan.py` `frame_fits`: the picture checker now also answers NO to blurry, out-of-focus, washed-out or almost black frames. Mild blur is left to that vision check.
